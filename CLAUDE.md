# Protein Crunch Mix – intro video (HyperFrames)

A 20-second product-launch intro that opens a school presentation of a snack: **Protein Crunch Mix**, roasted peanuts and pumpkin seeds in a crunchy egg-white and pea-powder coating, sold in a royal blue stand-up pouch (150 g) and a small 40 g pack. Client framed as Unilever; audience Gen Z 18–26. On-screen language is Czech. The video is built with HyperFrames (HTML + GSAP rendered to MP4), following the guide "Motion Graphics with Claude Code" by @damianodesu.

Read this file, then `NOTES-hyperframes-guide.md` (guide summary, brand facts, build history) and `docs/analysis.md` (the team's market and persona analysis) before changing copy or design.

## Hard rules for all copy

- Allowed facts, exactly as written: **22 g bílkovin na 100 g**, **8,5 g vlákniny na 100 g**, **bez umělých barviv a konzervantů**. Do not invent other claims.
- Never write **"vysoký obsah bílkovin"** or "high protein" (regulatory risk; reads as processed food). Never use the word **"těstíčko"** (at competitors it means flour and starch).
- Czech text keeps all diacritics. Headlines at least 120 px, all text inside the middle 80 % of the width (readable from the back of a classroom).
- Message order from the analysis: 1 taste and crunch, 2 protein and satiety, 3 short ingredient list. Tone: honest, concrete numbers, funny not preachy, never "fitness bro". Personas: Adam (gym student), Bára (office junior who runs), Sam (first-year, gaming and parties). Copy must speak to all three, not only athletes.
- Decided copy: hook "Svačina, která zasytí." (owner may still change it), slogan **"Křupe. / Zasytí. / Nic neskrývá."** (third line gold), subline "Křupavá směs oříšků a semínek.", CTA **"Měj energii vždy po ruce"** (final), sizes line "150 g · 40 g".

## Visual rules

- Colours: royal blue #1f2fb0, navy #121a6b, gold-to-orange #ffc928 → #f28c18, warm background gradient #f2a23a → #b5581b with soft light rays from the top left, white text. Font Nunito 900 for headlines, 700 for small text (`fonts/Nunito-Variable.ttf`, Czech glyphs verified).
- Brand motifs: the star window on the pack and the gold line-art peanuts, pumpkin seeds and nuts of the pack pattern. Background decoration uses exactly these (SVG symbols `la-*` in `index.html`). No almonds (not in the product). No hand-drawn filled "nut" illustrations: the owner rejected them as fake-looking.
- Product artwork: real cut-outs `assets/pouch-150.png` and `assets/pouch-40.png` (RGBA). Originals are the two `Gemini_Generated_Image_*.jpg` files; they are JPEGs with a fake checkerboard, cut by flood-filling the neutral grey from the border. The gold "light sweep" is a masked gradient bar over the image.
- Motion: energetic but premium. power3.out / power4.out entrances, back.out(1.6) only for icon pops, no spinning. Text rises and settles; no motion blur on text (it left ghost copies and was removed).
- Reference images: `reference/ad.jpeg` (print ad) and `reference/back.jpeg` (back of pouch).

## Structure of `index.html` (1920×1080, 20 s, 60 fps)

1. 0–3 s hook: gold stars and line-art pieces fly at the camera, headline rises in.
2. 3–7 s reveal: 150 g pouch rises with a tilt and lands (crumb and star burst), gold sweep over the wordmark, 40 g pack slides in behind on the right.
3. 7–14 s facts: pouch parks in the left third; three facts appear one at a time (count-ups for 22 and 8,5) and shrink into a checklist with gold outline icons and ticks.
4. 14–17 s slogan: gentle punch-in, three beats "Křupe. / Zasytí. / Nic neskrývá.", subline.
5. 17–20 s end card: wordmark (HTML text), both packs, yellow CTA pill, sizes. Everything is still from 18.5 s.

Sound: small synthesised effects in `assets/sfx/` (pop, tick, counter roll, thud, hit, crunch, sweep; generated by a numpy script, licence-free) plus HyperFrames' bundled chime. Cues are `<audio data-start data-volume>` elements at the bottom of `#root`. The owner dislikes the synthesised crunch; a real bite recording is wanted. `index-silent.html` and `index-nocrunch.html` are generated variants of `index.html` (all audio removed / crunch cues removed); regenerate them after editing `index.html` rather than editing them by hand.

Vendored: `vendor/gsap.min.js` (GSAP 3.14.2). `vendor/motion-blur.js` is no longer used.

## Commands

```bash
npx hyperframes lint
npx hyperframes check --at 1,4.2,6,10,13.9,15,19
npx hyperframes snapshot --at 2,5,10,16,19 --no-end --output .qc/frames
npx hyperframes render --fps 60 --quality delivery --workers 2 --output renders/intro.mp4
npx hyperframes render -c index-nocrunch.html --fps 60 --quality delivery --workers 2 --output renders/intro-nocrunch.mp4
```

Stills from a finished render: `ffmpeg -ss <t> -i renders/intro.mp4 -frames:v 1 renders/stills/still-<t>s.png`.

Machine setup (once per computer): Node 22+, FFmpeg, whisper.cpp on PATH, then `npx hyperframes skills` and restart Claude Code. `npx hyperframes doctor` confirms it; Docker, Kokoro and MusicGen are optional. Use `--workers 2` on machines with little free RAM; capturing many snapshots in one call can crash the headless browser, so take 4–6 frames per call.
